# Buy me a coffee

Support is voluntary and does not unlock functionality. No payment destination has been published yet. Once the maintainer confirms a public HTTPS support page, add it to `.github/FUNDING.yml` and `manifest.json` (`fundingUrl`). An optional bank transfer can be documented on the public support page with the recipient, IBAN/account, currency, suggested reference, and BIC/SWIFT if needed. The plugin only opens a link; it does not initiate payments or handle banking credentials.
